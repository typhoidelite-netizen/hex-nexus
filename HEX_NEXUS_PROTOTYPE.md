# Hex Nexus Prototype 0.1

Hex Nexus is a private 2-4 player Commander tabletop client built on the XMage rules engine.

Prototype scope:
- preserved XMage rules and card identifiers;
- private-server defaults;
- first-pass Hex Nexus client and server branding;
- no card renaming or reskinning;
- no protocol or deck-format changes.

XMage attribution and license terms remain applicable to the underlying engine.
